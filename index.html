Hisab Khata (Money Manager)

হিসাব খাতা — আয় ও ব্যয়ের সহজ হিসাব

A simple money manager for Android. Track income and expenses across wallets, set budgets and regular payments, get reminders, and back up to your own Google Drive. Available in Bangla and English.

  • Your data stays on your phone, encrypted, with optional Google Drive backup
  • Reports, budgets and PDF/CSV export
  • Reminders for bills and regular payments
