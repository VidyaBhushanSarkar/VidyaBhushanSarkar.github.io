Privacy Policy for Hisab Khata (Money Manager)

Effective Date: 10th November, 2026

Developer: Vidya Bhushan Sarkar

Contact: thedigitive@gmail.com

Hisab Khata (Money Manager) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Hisab Khata (Money Manager) is a personal finance app designed to store your financial information on your device.

We may process the following types of information:

  • Financial records you enter (such as transactions, wallets, categories, budgets, regular payments, reminders, and notes).
  • Receipt photos you choose to attach to a transaction.
  • App security settings (such as PIN or biometric lock preferences).
  • If you sign in with Google for backup: your Google account name, email address, and profile photo, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Basic technical data required for app functionality (for example, app version or device compatibility).
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob, described in Section 6.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

  • Your financial data is stored locally on your device.
  • Data is protected using encryption.
  • Receipt photos are stored in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • We do not store your personal financial data on our servers. We do not operate any servers that receive your data.

3. Google Drive Backup (Optional)

You can choose to back up your data to your own Google Drive by signing in with your Google account.

  • Backups are saved in a hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive. The app requests access only to this folder (the drive.appdata permission). It cannot see, read, or change any other files in your Google Drive.
  • A backup contains your financial records, settings, and receipt photos.
  • If automatic sync is turned on, the app updates this backup when your data changes.
  • We (the developer) cannot access your Google Drive or your backups.
  • Data stored in Google Drive is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can turn off sync, delete backups from the app’s Backup screen, or sign out at any time.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial data with third parties.

Your data remains on your device unless you choose to export, backup, or share it yourself, for example:

  • Backing up to your own Google Drive (Section 3).
  • Exporting a PDF or CSV report and sharing it using your device’s share options.

The only third-party service that receives data from the app on its own is Google AdMob, which receives advertising data (Section 6). AdMob never receives your financial records.

5. Permissions and Security Features

Depending on your usage, the app may request access to:

  • Biometric authentication / device credentials — to unlock the app securely.
  • Camera and photos — only when you choose to attach a receipt photo. Photos are picked through Android’s system camera or photo picker.
  • Notifications — to show reminders for bills and regular payments.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) — so reminders arrive at the time you set.
  • Run at startup — to restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration — for reminder notifications.
  • Internet and network state — for Google Drive backup, showing ads, and downloading fonts used in PDF reports.

These permissions are used only to provide app functionality and improve security.

6. Advertising

Hisab Khata (Money Manager) shows ads using Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google.

  • AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about the ads you see and tap. This is used to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud.
  • Your financial records, receipt photos, and notes are never shared with AdMob or used for advertising.
  • Where the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice from the app.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Learn more about how Google uses data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

7. Analytics

Hisab Khata (Money Manager) does not use analytics or crash-reporting tools, and does not track your personal financial entries.

8. Other Network Requests

When you create a PDF report, the app downloads Bengali fonts from Google Fonts. This request contains no personal or financial data, but, like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to Google.

9. Children’s Privacy

Hisab Khata (Money Manager) is not specifically directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Because data is stored locally on your device, you control how long it is kept:

  • You can delete individual records (such as transactions, wallets, budgets, and reminders) at any time from inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all local app data from your device. The app turns off Android’s automatic device backup, so this data is not copied elsewhere by Android.
  • Google Drive backups stay in your Google Drive until you delete them. You can delete them from the app’s Backup screen, or in Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → Hisab Khata (Money Manager) → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Data collected by AdMob is kept by Google according to Google’s own policies.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights related to your personal data (such as access, correction, or deletion).

Since your data is stored on your device and in your own Google Drive, you generally control your data directly through the app, your device, and your Google account. For advertising data, you can use the controls described in Section 6. For any other request, contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updates will be posted on this page, within the app, and/or on the app listing with a revised effective date.
